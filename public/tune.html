What this page does

Lets someone tune the X1PRO's feed from elsewhere. They send test batches; this phone runs each one with the motion settings they ask for, for that batch only, sends every test card to the reject bin, and photographs where each card landed. The machine's saved settings are never changed from here. Each setting can move at most 30% from its saved value, and a test is at most 20 cards.
